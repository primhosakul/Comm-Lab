In Public

Avoiding awkwardness or Interaction with others

Step 1: Use props

  • Hold a drink
  • Check your phone
  • Look at something around you (menus, magazines)
  • Wear headphones

Step 2: Purposeful phone check

  • Scroll with intent
  • Stop after 20-30 seconds
  • Look like you are checking somethings

Step 3: If walking, walk with a purpose

  • Avoid wandering
  • Pick a target (go to a specific place)
