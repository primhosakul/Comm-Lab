In Class

When you finshed early...

Step 1: Stay physically engaged

  • Keep your notebook open
  • Hold a pen
  • Keep your laptop screen active

Step 2: Do low effort tasks

  • Review your notes
  • Flick through pages (pretend you're reading)
  • Engage with your laptop

Step 3: Maintain focused body language

  • Neutral facial expression
  • Look at your page/screen (not around the room)
  • Engage with your laptop

Step 4: Deliberate movements

  • Turn pages at the right time
  • Type at a steady pace
