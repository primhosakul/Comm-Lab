Hey Prim,

i still can’t believe i’m actually leaving. everything’s happening so fast that it doesn’t even feel real yet. my room is half empty now and there are boxes everywhere, and it’s weird seeing all these pieces of my life packed up like they’re nothing. i thought i’d be more excited, but honestly i think i’m mostly scared.

i keep thinking about all the little things i’m going to miss. staying out too late for no reason, sitting on your bedroom floor talking about everything and nothing, music playing in the background while we complained about growing up like it was never actually going to happen. i didn’t realise those moments would become memories so quickly. everyone keeps saying this is a “new beginning,” but no one talks about how hard it is to leave behind a version of yourself that only existed with certain people. i think that’s the part i hate the most. knowing things are going to change no matter how badly i want them to stay the same.

i’m scared we’ll drift apart one day without noticing. like eventually we’ll stop sending each other random photos or texting about stupid things that happened during the day. i know that probably sounds dramatic, but lately everything feels temporary and it’s terrifying. but even with all of that, i’m really grateful for you. you made this place feel like home to me. and no matter where i end up, i think part of me will always stay in these moments with you. promise me you won’t forget me, okay?

Love,
